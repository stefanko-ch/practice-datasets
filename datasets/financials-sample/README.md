# Financials – Excel workbook with two sheets (synthetic)

Sheet `Financials1`: monthly budget/actuals per account and business unit (wide format, Jan–Dec). Sheet `Financials2`: 700 sales transactions. Deliberately messy like real exports: the header typo `Businees Unit`, the column ` Sales` with a leading space, the year stored as text.
